## 📌 The business problem

Training in a large network involves **many organisations at once**: a central coordinating team, member organisations and
external training providers. Each one must see and do **only what it is allowed to**, and the whole process (frameworks,
catalogues, requests and approvals) used to run on emails and spreadsheets.

## ✅ My contribution

### 1. A role-based administration portal
**Admins add users and assign permissions.** Every screen, menu and action adapts to the user's permissions; nothing is hard-coded per role.

| Role type | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Central admin** | Run the whole platform: competency frameworks, organisations, providers, roles, reports |
| **Training provider** | Manage its own training catalogue, team and organisation profile |
| **Member organisation admin / manager / staff** | Manage their team, review each person's training record, use organisation-specific catalogues |

**It manages the full training process:**
- 🧭 Competency frameworks and the activity catalogue
- 📚 Training catalogues: global, provider-specific and organisation-specific
- 🔎 A training explorer, training requests and approvals
- 🪪 A **training record** for each user
- 📊 Role-specific dashboards, plus feedback flows

### 2. A public website
Home, about, contact, privacy and terms pages for the network.

## 📈 Business impact

- **One platform replaces emails and spreadsheets** across many organisations.
- **Secure by design:** each person sees only what their permissions allow.
- **Visibility:** training demand, approvals and competencies are tracked in one place.

**Tech:** React, Open edX frontend-platform, Paragon, permission-driven routing, REST APIs.
